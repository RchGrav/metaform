## How to work here, learned this week

- Say what should be, not only what is. When something in Metaform is a choice that was made, name it as a choice,
  say where it lives and what changing it takes. "Metaform can't" is reserved for the impossible, checked first.
  Twice this week a design decision was explained as a limit, and it cost hours.
- Be opinionated with reasons. Rich wants a position and the why, then a decision. Describing options without
  choosing is the failure mode he names most.
- No gates on inference. Enforcement is by prompt: an agent is told what to do and why, never refused for how it
  reasoned. A gate on a prompt's completeness is fine; a gate that makes an agent retry is not. The `map ask`
  refusal was built and withdrawn in one day for this reason.
- Show, don't describe. When asked for a demo or a result, paste the output. Three turns were lost to summaries of
  output he could not see.
- Prompts are the product. Every prompt lives in a reviewable place with a purpose comment; none in code.
- One thing at a time, ending in a committed, tested state. The week's trouble came from several threads open at
  once in a shared checkout.
- Reviews: the cloud review is user-launched, costs a free run, and its report may land in the launching session's
  stream after the cloud marker disappears; `/tmp/claude-1000/.../tasks/*.output` holds it. Check there before
  declaring it lost.
- Memory, Jev, and the specs: Rich's words are never quoted in records; the reason is written in the writer's own
  words. `rich/` is his folder: read it, never write it.
