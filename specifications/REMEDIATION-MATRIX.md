# Metaform remediation and compliance matrix

**Status: active remediation. Boundary repairs and current evidence are recorded below; overall acceptance remains incomplete.**  
**Governing source: [Superseding Product Requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md), effective 2026-09-18.**

This matrix implements [Section 20.1](REQUIREMENTS.md#201-use-the-conformance-audit-as-the-defect-inventory) and [Section 20.3](REQUIREMENTS.md#203-keep-the-matrix-live). Its requirement axis contains only the 146 actual `REQ-*` IDs in that document. The defect axis accounts for all 42 numbered M/P/B/D findings and 13 C capability entries in the dated conformance audit, plus two newly explicit verification gaps. Journey IDs below identify verification work; they are not additional product requirements.

The governing requirements replace the previous freeze and older requirement inventories. Historical plans, proposed remedies and tests supply evidence only. In particular, the audit's older requirement labels, prescribed UI layouts, field names and remediation order are not normative here. The matrix follows the seven outside-in phases of [Section 20.2](REQUIREMENTS.md#202-remediate-outside-in), including substantive persistence, workflow, knowledge and polish work after the boundary repairs.
