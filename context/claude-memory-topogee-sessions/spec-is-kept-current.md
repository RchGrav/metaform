`TOPOGEE-SPEC.md` is kept current. When the founder settles something, it goes into the spec, so the spec always says what the system is. Something the founder only asked about, or is still thinking through, is recorded as open, or not at all.

**Why:** On 2026-11-02 an agent wrote the founder's questions into the spec as decisions (R-4.7 marked Decided from a "right?"). The founder objected to that. A later summary turned the objection into "nothing goes into the spec unless he says", and the spec was then left stale through a night of settled design. The objection was to decisions being invented, not to the spec being updated. The founder also said the spec comes first until it is wrong. When it is wrong and that blocks work, do it right, document it, and tell the founder right away.

**How to apply:** Before writing a status, check that the founder settled it rather than asked it. If unsure whether something is settled, record it as open. Do not use the spec, or any memory, as a reason to stop doing the work. See [[owner-files-reference]].
