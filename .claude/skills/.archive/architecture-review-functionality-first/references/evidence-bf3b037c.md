Correction to your brief: functionality comes first; requirements are secondary. Judge each part by whether the product works for the person: fast, reliable, cheap in tokens, doing what it is for. A specification record that blocks or degrades working functionality is wrong and gets refined, with its why. Many records were written by agents and stamped as the owner's (CTX-020 was an agent's paraphrase), so a record is evidence of intent, not a rule to defend. Structure findings around working functionality: what must work, what blocks it, what the foundation must be; where a record conflicts with it, say so and propose the refined record.

Also: the specifications are dated and many records have been overtaken since they were written. Do not take a record as current. Check it against what is newer: CLAUDE.md as it reads now, the owner's own recent commits (git log, 2026-10-19 and 20...), docs/reviews/2026-10-19-api-cost-and-token-waste-audit.md. Where a record is overtaken, say so and name what replaced it.
