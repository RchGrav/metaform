# Traceable findings

Identify the reviewed work, candidate and requirement revision. Describe the concrete scenario, affected receiving purpose, observed behavior, evidence and consequence. Label a supported inference or uncertainty accordingly. Give a remedy and verification case when established; retain a demonstrated defect even when its remedy needs investigation.

Reconcile newer direction against the specific finding. An authorized change can supersede a requirement without erasing a different defect in the same component. Search related work and history before duplicating it. Shared wording or a title match is a search hint, not proof of equivalence.

Return findings once through the recorded review route. Let its correction operation retain and route them. Propose distinct new outcomes explicitly rather than manufacturing a correction ticket for every observation.
