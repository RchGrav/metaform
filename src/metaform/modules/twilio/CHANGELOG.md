# Changelog

Each change to the twilio module, newest first.

## Unreleased

- The phone bridge reads the system agent's thread again on a change to dispatch's `dispatch.session_lines` and `dispatch.agent_sessions` too, where dispatch keeps the conversations since its tables version 16, so the person's calls follow their agent after a clear or a restore of the map, 2026-10-16.

- Quiet start and a direct route, 2026-10-15, ticket bd730760 (system-review remediation of 0a044360): the check prints nothing and the page shows no phone banner; its findings are in `twilio.log`, `check.json`, `twilio check` and `twilio status`. `web start` with the phone off runs no check. A problem found by a check nobody asked for, once the person has set up a number, credentials or a public address, is raised to the person's system agent as dispatch's `setup-not-working` signal, once per set of findings (`run/twilio/raised.json`), again only when they change or a day after nobody took it up. `twilio_status` and `twilio_check` are MCP tools again for the person's own AI tool and their system agent once the phone is set up; `fix=true` stays the person's, refused over MCP. New `twilio_bind` and `twilio_certificate` serve the bridge on an address a router forwards a port to, with TLS from a PEM; the check probes such a direct route for a closed port, a TLS failure or a wrong certificate, and says when WSL2's NAT networking keeps the port from Linux.
- Phone self-check, 2026-10-15, ticket 0a044360: `metaform twilio check` reads where Twilio sends the number's calls and texts (a GET that changes nothing), compares them with the address the bridge serves and asks whether it answers there, and says each thing wrong with its one fix: an address that does not answer, a mismatch, the bridge not running, no `twilio_public_url`, a TwiML App in the way; a temporary quick tunnel is flagged. `fix=true`, the person's own, points the number's voice and SMS addresses at the bridge, and refuses when the bridge does not answer there. It runs once the bridge listens and at `web.started`; its report is kept in `run/twilio/check.json`, logged, given by the module's new status hook as a notice for `metaform status` and the web page's banner, and shown in `twilio status` as `check`. The run file records the public address the bridge serves. Found when Rich's number answered every call with Twilio's "application error": Twilio sent its calls to a closed trycloudflare.com tunnel and nothing said so.
- Settings cleanup, 2026-10-15, ticket 0d9e3882: `twilio_port` is the constant `PORT`, 7441, the port a Node's Caddy routes `/phone/*` to; a port another program holds is said at `serve` with the next step. `twilio_language` merges into the voice module's `voice_language`, so the phone and the page hear one language; a value stored in the old key is carried there once.
- The twilio module: your own Twilio number answered by your system agent, your mascot, speaking, and calls or texts when an agent waits on you, looks stuck, reaches its budget or the intake finishes, as you choose; the verbs `status`, `serve`, `call`, `text` and `test`; its settings `twilio_*`; the bridge on `127.0.0.1:7441`, reached by Twilio at `/phone/voice`, `/phone/pin`, `/phone/sms` and `/phone/stream`; the caller's proof by STIR/SHAKEN attestation or `METAFORM_TWILIO_PIN`. Built as `phone` and renamed for the service it reaches before any release, so nothing is migrated: `phone_*` settings are `twilio_*`, `phone_twilio_number` is `twilio_number`, `METAFORM_PHONE_PIN` is `METAFORM_TWILIO_PIN`, and the run folder is `<home>/run/twilio`.
- `twilio_voice` speaks: an ElevenLabs voice id, or a voice's name on the account, passed to the voice module's `speak` as `voice`; empty, the mascot character's voice.
- The system agent's answer is polled with dispatch `sessions` `since`, so each poll reads only the thread's lines after the person's words.
