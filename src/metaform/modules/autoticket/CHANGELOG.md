# Changelog

Each change to the module, newest first.

## Unreleased

- 2026-10-19, change order e7f51c88 from the functional assessment of code.scheduler.task#9: the watch starts when the fix runs, at once in the project folder or once its worktree merged (dispatch's new `work.in_force`), the ticket saying `Fix landed, waiting to merge` until then; each `fault.raised` from the web server, the page, the MCP server and the runner says when its code was loaded, `loaded`, and a recurrence from code loaded before the fix came into force, or while it waits to merge, is noted with what puts the fix in force rather than giving the ticket back; a proven close keeps its landing, so old code raising after it reopens nothing; a named test not in the project folder is said in the note.
- 2026-10-19, ticket 3c444f28 (code.scheduler.task#9), a fix is proven before its ticket closes: each ticket it files carries condition broken, its fault light, and closes only with proof (the map's `prove_work`): an agent's done lands the fix, `Fix landed, watching`, and the ticket stays open until its group has not recurred for `autoticket_quiet_minutes` (new setting, 30) of the project's work, one timer per watched ticket in the new `proof.py` background service, or a test named in the resolution, `test: <file>::<name>`, passes. A recurrence while watched gives the ticket back with the fix that did not hold; one after close reopens the same ticket with that fix and the new evidence, kind and light broken, closing only with proof again. A watched ticket takes no slot under `autoticket_open_most` (`holds` is now `resting`). No table change: the counted seconds are `state` rows.
- 2026-10-18, ticket 6cba466b (code.scheduler.task#d), a failed MCP verb names its ticket: the `fault.raised` handler's result gains `tickets`, the tickets the pass filed its faults as or counted them on (empty while they wait for a slot, absent when another pass took them), so the MCP server's error answer says the failure is filed for a fix, naming the ticket, instead of asking for `metaform_feedback`, which filed the same failure again as a held ticket.
- 2026-10-18, ticket 51255288 (code.scheduler.task#c), held tickets take no slot: `autoticket_open_most` counts only the open tickets it filed that are not held, read from each ticket when it counts, so held feedback and data-removal tickets no longer stop later faults from being filed; a filed ticket put on hold frees its slot at once.
- 2026-10-18, ticket bf47336a (code.scheduler.task#8), faults file themselves: the module subscribes to `fault.raised`, published by the web server's faults, the page's errors (the new `/api/page-faults`), a failed MCP verb, `metaform_feedback` and a dispatch run that crashed or whose agent exited with its ticket open, and to `map.changed`. A new kind of fault or log error is filed at once as a ticket of kind broken at the element owning its file (the nearest owner when no ref names it); a recurrence rewrites its count and last seen in that ticket's note; at most `autoticket_open_most` (5) are open, the rest waiting until one is done; an agent's error on an autoticket ticket is counted there; feedback and data-removal errors are filed held with why. Faults group by their place (source, file, function) and their message. On by default (`SHIPS_DISABLED = False`). Removed: the timed ranked list and its `autoticket_interval_minutes` setting, the baseline, the `autoticket-list` and `autoticket-new` signals, and the `reset` and `promote` verbs. Tables at version 2: groups gain `source` and `reproduce` and lose `listed` and `known`. New prompts `ticket`, `held_design` and `held_data`, wording for the person's approval.
- 2026-10-15, ticket 2ede3067 (code.system.management.knowledge#32), no polling: a local log at its end is followed through inotify (Linux) or kqueue (macOS) rather than looked at every 0.25 s; the watch sleeps until an event or a real deadline rather than turning every 0.25 s; a lease is a lock file per log, waited on in a thread and taken over the moment its holder ends, rather than a beat written every second and looked at every 2 s; counts and positions are written only after something was read; a log added or removed elsewhere, or a rule, reaches a running watch through `map.changed`. No table change: `holder` and `beat` stay, as who reads the log and when it last wrote.
- The module, from the design in `artifacts/specs-draft/autoticket.md`, ticket 0fc3c02b: watched logs, local or over ssh, read as they grow and never kept; entries joined from their lines; errors grouped by onhold's FNV-1a and Dice method at 0.45; one example per group with secrets masked; a lease per log so two processes never count one line twice; the ranked list as a signal every `autoticket_interval_minutes`, the first setting the baseline, and a group first seen after it reported at once; `promote` to make a group a ticket. Tables at version 1: `groups`, `logs`, `rules`, `state`. The intake step and the connect notice are read from prompts.xml once their wording is approved.
