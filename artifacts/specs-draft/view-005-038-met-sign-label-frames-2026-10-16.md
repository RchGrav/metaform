## VIEW-005: append after the amendment of 2026-10-15 (stretched label, ticket code.interface.graphical#63)

That amendment lists the ways a meeting ends other than a hand or a flight: V or E, and a memory network opening. It does not cover the sign itself moving while the camera stays still. This bullet adds that case and leaves the earlier bullet as written.

- Amendment, 2026-10-16, met sign leaves square on, ticket code.interface.graphical#82: a meeting also ends when its sign is no longer square on to the eye or has moved on the screen from where it was met: more than 1 degree off square, or its projected centre more than 2 pixels from where it was first drawn. The end is the same as a hand on the camera: the sign goes back into the scene and the near plane eases back. The overlay draws the sign at the rectangle its four corners project to, which matches the sign only while it is square on. Before this change, when a layout change moved the met node while the camera stayed still (the layout heating again after any change to the map), the meeting stayed on: the sign turned up to 89 degrees off square, its name was drawn up to twice its width, and the name slid off the top of the view. In the small area the camera follows the met node, `holdMet`, so the sign stays square and the meeting holds. Re-placing the large camera on the moved sign was considered and set aside: it would move the view without a hand, which VIEW-041 rules out. Build: `metSign` in `page/app/map3d/labels.js` (`SQUARE`, `STRAY`), ending through `meetEnds` in `camera/fly3d.js`. Acceptance: a node met in Zoom mode in the large area, then `G.d3ReheatSimulation()`, sampled every 0.5 s for 6 s. Every sample has no meeting, or a stretch of 0.98 to 1.02 at under 1 degree off square. Measured on the scratch probe `/tmp/claude-1000/t82/scripts/probe_met.py`: before, the meeting stays on at 59 degrees off square with the overlay at y -1213; after, the meeting ends at the first sample.

## VIEW-038: append at the end of the record (after its Build line and the amendment of 2026-10-10)

The record's Build sentence "Each frame `layered` puts each label in place and marks one that falls on a nearer node's sphere or on a nearer label as under" is no longer exact. This bullet supersedes that one sentence and leaves the Build line as written.

- Amendment, 2026-10-16, label frames, ticket code.interface.graphical#82: this supersedes the Build sentence "Each frame `layered` puts each label in place and marks one that falls on a nearer node's sphere or on a nearer label as under". `layered` places the labels again only in a frame where something they are placed from changed: the camera's matrices, a node's place, the area's size, the pixel ratio, the selection and its neighbours, the reach, the node under the pointer, the meeting, the small area, the shown labels, the physics preset, the sign mode, or the theme's colours and face. A name or a record changed in nodes kept in place (`inPlace` in `draw3d.js`) calls `relabel()`, and while a join's title fades or a ghost rises the labels are placed every frame. Every frame is still drawn; only identical results are not worked out again. No frame asks the page for a size: the 3D area's size is kept by an observer of `#stage` (`STAGE` in `graph3d.js`), the 2D area's width by an observer of `#viewport` (`roam2d.js`), and whether the 2D map is in view by an observer of its drawing (`watch2d` in `flow2d.js`, in place of `checkVisibility`). Why: with the camera still, the labels cost 1.26 ms a frame for 397 nodes, and the size reads and `checkVisibility` forced a second layout every frame. Measured with three working figures, flow all and the camera still, on the app-internal-refactor map: `layered` went from 1.26 to 0.023 ms a frame, layouts from 2.0 to 1.0 a frame, forced layouts from 93 to 0 in 4 s, and `prepare3d` from 3.5 to 1.3 ms. A forced full pass after each of seven changes (settle, select, hover, theme to black and back, a map change in place, clearing the selection) left 0 of 168 labels different from the skipped frames.
