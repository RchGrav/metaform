### VIEW-051 Depth: key 4 sets how many levels of the map show, and a click breaks out a node's next level in place

- Requirement: The setting `web_map_depth` is 1, 2, 3, 4 or all, and the key 4 steps through those values in that order and round again. Levels are counted along the joins from the top: the CQUs and every other element nothing receives from are level 1, what serves them is level 2, and so on. A part's level is its shortest way down, so a part that serves two receivers shows when either one does. A part deeper than the depth stays inside the receiver it is first reached through, and that receiver's name on both maps ends with `+N`, the count of every part held beneath it. Both maps re-fit after each change. A click on a node that holds parts selects it and breaks out its next level in place on the same map. A click on it again folds that level back in, with anything opened beneath it. Changing the depth folds every node that was opened by a click. Enter alone still opens the inside view, VIEW-016. Both maps, 3D and 2D, draw the same parts.
- What this means:
  - The default is all, so a map looks as it did until 4 is pressed. The setting is global, like `web_flow_mode`. Each press is said at the 2D map's corner: `Depth: 1 level`, `Depth: 2 levels`, `Depth: all levels`.
  - A hidden part takes everything drawn on it with it: its purpose vertices, the layer items and memories hung on it, and its namespace link in 3D. A join shows only while its receiver and at least one of its providers show.
  - An element reached from no top, such as one only on a cycle, counts as a top of its own, as `roots()` orders the walk of the keys, VIEW-008.
  - Clicking a node that holds nothing works as before: a click on the selected node goes inside it, VIEW-008. A click that breaks out or folds a node never goes inside.
  - A part chosen while it is held is broken out so it can be seen: every receiver on its way down from the top is opened as clicks would open it. This holds wherever the choice comes from: the search, a link, the arrow keys (Down onto a held part), or an agent's `web_select`. A join chosen while held opens its receiver.
  - An agent at work on a held part is drawn at the node that holds it, on both maps.
  - The inside view and a memory network show every level. The depth applies only to the whole map.
  - The upstream and downstream toggles, VIEW-046, still apply on top of the depth.
- Why: a large map drawn whole is too dense to read, and going inside a node, VIEW-016, swaps the map for one node's own graph and drops the context around it. Holding the deeper levels inside the parts they serve keeps the top of the map readable. Breaking a node out in place lets the person go down one branch while the rest of the map stays where it was. The count says what is held, so nothing is hidden without a sign. Folding everything at a new depth means a depth always shows exactly that depth. Revealing a chosen part keeps every way of choosing a part working at any depth.
- Build: `DEPTH`, `depthView`, `holderOf`, `stepDepth`, `setDepth`, `toggleDepth` and `revealDepth` in `page/app/keys/walk.js`. `graphData` (`graph/data.js`) and `build2D` (`map2d/build.js`) draw only `depthView().shown`, named through `heldName`. `clicked2d` (`map2d/flow2d.js`) and `clicked3d` (`map3d/pick3d.js`) call `toggleDepth`. `select` (`selection/select.js`) calls `revealDepth`. Figures find their node through `holderOf` (`figures/agents.js`, `figures/roam2d.js`). `followView` (`views/settings.js`) reads the setting. The setting is declared in `modules/web/__init__.py`, and the key is listed in `KEYS` (`keys/keys.js`).
- Acceptance: `test_4_sets_the_depth_and_a_click_breaks_out_a_nodes_next_level_in_place_on_both_maps`. At depth 1 the cook alone of its chain shows, named `+3`, in both maps. A click on it shows the timer and the tick without going inside, and a click again folds them. Choosing the alarm while it is held shows it. A new depth folds what was opened, and the setting keeps it. Enter still goes inside.
