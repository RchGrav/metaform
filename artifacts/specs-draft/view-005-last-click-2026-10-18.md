# VIEW-005 amendment draft: the person's last click, told to their system agent

Draft for the owner, from ticket code.interface.graphical#124 (2026-10-18). Not in `specifications/` until approved.

Proposed bullet after VIEW-005's `Status:` line in `specifications/09-viewer.md`:

- Amendment, 2026-10-18, ticket code.interface.graphical#124, the page keeps the last thing the person clicked, so their system agent knows what "this" is: its kind (element, join, agent figure, board card, banner, notice, chat card, control, panel section), what names it (address and name for a map part, ticket item and agent for a figure or card, the visible label for a control or notice), the state it showed with the open ticket behind it, and when. `web_viewing` returns it as `clicked`. The person's first message to their system agent within two minutes of the click, through the chat or asked of their mascot, starts with one line naming it, and their bubble shows that line as a chip; a later message to the system agent, or one sent after two minutes, carries none, while a message to another agent leaves it untold. The chat's own head, tabs, buttons and message box, a Chat button, the mascot and its ask field are not recorded, since a click that opens the chat must not replace what the person asks about; nothing typed is recorded. A join is recorded as a join, clicked on its edge, since it has no node of its own.
