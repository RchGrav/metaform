- Amendment, 2026-10-21, ticket code.dispatcher.agent#10, recorded to match the code: the head agent fans a job out through Metaform itself, with no mechanism of its own. (1) A plan is tickets filed with `map work op=add`, each at its Address with its agent, model and effort (BASE-066) and the tickets it waits on, `after_work` (09-viewer, amendments of 2026-10-17); the Planner role writes them in their order and routes each through `orchestration_route`. No single verb files a whole plan: one call per ticket puts each on the board and the map as it is filed. (2) The agent starter starts no ticket while one it waits on is open, starts what a done ticket freed on that ticket's `map.changed`, and runs up to `dispatch_agents` at once; each ticket works in its own worktree and merges into main through the merge module with the person's review. (3) `orchestration_assess` opens a `Functional assessment of <id>` ticket for the functionality role in another session, bounded by `orchestration_reviews`; a finding reopens the reviewed ticket or files a corrective ticket at its node. (4) A child assignment's result returns to its caller as a note `<item> complete`; a root claim's result goes to the Project updates conversation, and routine progress and completion send no message to the system agent (the amendment of 2026-10-21 above). When the last ticket of a plan is done, one project update of kind `plan_report` says so, once for the plan: its tickets by outcome, from its first ticket to the one just done; it is heard on the `done` kind the map says beside `ticket` on a ticket's end, and it wakes the system agent, so the person hears the job is done.
