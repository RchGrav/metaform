- Amendment, 2026-10-16, ticket 2cbdeb480d964c6190966b5b9060d8f3 (code.interface.graphical#9e): the person's mascot is one record, the global setting `web_mascot`, held by the server. It is the body of the person's system agent in every conversation and on every page, drawn while a conversation with the system agent runs, and dispatch's `mascot_line` names the same character in the system agent's prompt. Giving the system agent a body in the chat, from the offer, its avatar or B, sets `web_mascot`, since that choice is the person's; the page is told of a change made anywhere (another page, the Settings screen, `metaform set`, an agent) by the `map.changed` that names `web.settings`, reads the settings once for a burst, and draws the mascot as it is now. While `web_mascot` is set, the chat never offers the system agent a body; `web_embody` offer and always apply only while it is none, and always then gives the first character free, which becomes the mascot. The system agent may take a body of its own with `web_embody` for one conversation, its own figure, and the mascot is not drawn while it walks; asking for the mascot's own character keeps it the person's. Bodies of other conversation agents are kept for their conversation in the browser, as before. Why: three records named the mascot and nothing tied them: `web_mascot`, read by the prompt and by the page only as the character suggested first; the body given in the chat, kept in the browser per conversation and dropped for a new one; and the last body picked, kept in the browser and put first under `web_embody` always. A new conversation asked again, and the chat could show an owl while the prompt said crystal. The two browser copies are removed. Build: `preferredGlyph`, `mascotGlyph` in `page/app/figures/mascot.js`; `bodyOf`, `ownBody`, `embody` in `page/app/figures/agents.js`; `followMascot`, `mascotHeard`, `keepMascot` in `page/app/views/settings.js`; `embodyOffer`, `embodyAlways` in `page/app/dock/embody.js`.
