- Amendment, 2026-10-14: only the sentinel and the head agent ask the person. A question any other session puts through ACP's elicitation, a worker's, an intake's or a merge preparation's, never reaches the person. Dispatch answers it at once with the `decide` prompt in each own-words field: the agent is to decide with its best judgment, record the choice and its why in the ticket with `map_work op=step`, and go on. A form with no own-words field or with a required field, and a page to open, are declined. Nothing is written to the node, the thread, the signals or the chat. `dispatch_elicitation` applies only to the sessions of the agents the person summons: the sentinel, a talk and the system agent, the runner started with `system=`; a roundtable seat still declines, BASE-066. Why: asking was meant to be gone with `map ask`, MAP-050, but elicitation kept a way for workers to reach the person; agents raised hundreds of questions and used them as a reason not to work. The person meets purpose-defined agents only, VIEW-045.
