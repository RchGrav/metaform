## Bullet

- Amendment, 2026-10-15, speech bubbles and the agent view's ease, ticket code.interface.graphical#81 (d1eb95faadb24f0f8a2f9309a7e86d2a), built by a Claude agent on that ticket and recorded to match the code: each agent at work shows the newest line it said in its thread as a comic speech bubble beside its figure in the 3D map. The bubble faces the camera, is the same size on the screen at any distance (key 5 steps through five sizes), and is drawn over the map. A new line shows for `web_bubble_seconds`, 5 by default, and the pointer on an agent brings its last line back. The bubbles are placed nearest agent first. Each one stays where it stands if it is free; otherwise it goes just above, below, right or left of a bubble already placed, whichever overlaps no other bubble and moves it least. Every bubble is kept inside the view, 1 percent in from each edge. With no free place it takes the one that overlaps the least, and the nearer bubble is drawn over it. An agent behind the camera shows no bubble; one beside the view has its words at the view's edge. A bubble is dimmer the farther its agent's head is than the head of the nearest agent talking, as (nearest / distance)^1.5, never under a third, so a bubble moved apart is as bright as its agent is near. Bubbles fade in and out by the clock, with a time constant of 84 ms, so they take the same time at any frame rate. In the agent view, V, the followed agent's body is hidden, so its own bubble is drawn at the bottom middle of the view at full brightness and over the others, for as long as it has a line. The agent view, V and E, eases in and back in 0.9 s by the clock, timed from the press. Leaving it gives the person's camera controls back at once. A hand on the camera while the view eases back puts the camera where it was at once, with the person's near plane, and the hand moves it from there. Before: bubbles moved only upward and went off the top of the view; they were dimmed by their distance after being moved and measured against the orbit point; fades and the eye ease advanced a fixed share each frame, so at 2 to 7 frames a second a bubble took 2.3 s to reach full and V off held the controls for 2.4 to 7.3 s; the followed agent's bubble was hidden with its body. Build: `bubbles3d`, `apart`, `atBottom` and `WATCHED` in `page/app/figures/bubble3d.js`; `eyesOf`, `eyes3d`, `backHome` and `handBack` in `page/app/figures/eyes.js`.
